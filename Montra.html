Our Story

At Montra Thai Massage, located at 6611 Kirby Dr in Houston, we bring the ancient healing traditions of Thailand to the heart of Houston. With over 15 years of experience, our skilled therapists are dedicated to providing authentic Thai massage and spa services that promote physical wellness and mental tranquility.

Our mission is to create a sanctuary where you can escape the stress of daily life and experience the transformative power of therapeutic touch. Every treatment is personalized to meet your unique needs, whether you seek relief from muscle tension, stress reduction, or simply a moment of peace.
